F1 101.

A few terms that make watching a race easier.

01

QUALIFYING

A session that decides the starting order for the Grand Prix.

02

PIT STOP

A short stop in the pit lane, usually to change tyres or make repairs.

03

SAFETY CAR

A car that leads the field at a controlled speed when the track needs to be safe.

04

UNDERCUT

A strategy where a driver pits earlier to try to gain position.

05

DNF

Did Not Finish. The driver did not complete the race.
